    FIELD NOTES / LAST ENTRY

    A mission ends.
    A trace remains.

    On the Moon and Mars, hardware becomes part of the landscape: a scientific instrument, a landing stage, a rover that can no longer answer. These objects are not all the same kind of “abandoned.” Some were left by design; some outlived their working lives; some ended in failure, and some locations remain uncertain.

    Look closely and the archive becomes more than a list of machines. It is a record of engineering choices, scientific questions, risk, and the work it takes to reach another world.
